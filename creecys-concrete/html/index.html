About Creecy's Concrete

We take the finish personally.

Concrete is unforgiving. Get the base, the steel or the timing wrong and it shows for the next thirty years. So we do the unglamorous part properly — set out accurately, prepare thoroughly, and don't rush the finish.

You'll get straight answers on what your job needs, a crew that turns up when we say we will, and a site left clean at the end of it.

  • Careful preparation Base, formwork and reinforcement sorted before a truck is booked.
  • Clean, square finish Level surfaces, tidy edges and joints cut where they belong.
  • Straightforward to deal with Clear quotes, clear timelines and a call if anything changes.
