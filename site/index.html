01 — About

A trade learned properly, and passed down.

Demasi Brothers and Sons is a family concreting business. The work is straightforward: prepare the ground properly, form it square, place the concrete well, and finish it so it still looks right in ten years.

Frank runs the jobs personally. That means one point of contact, honest advice about what a site actually needs, and a crew that turns up when it says it will.

We take on residential, commercial and civil work — from a single driveway through to a full slab-and-footings package.

The Demasi family

02 What we do

Four things, done properly.

We keep the list short on purpose. These are the jobs we do every week, and the ones we'll give you a straight answer on.

  • 01

    Driveways & Paths

    Plain, coloured or exposed aggregate. Set out to drain properly and jointed so it doesn't crack where it shouldn't.

  • 02

    Slabs & Footings

    Residential and commercial slabs, footings and foundations — formed, reinforced and poured to engineering specification.

  • 03

    Decorative Finishes

    Exposed aggregate, honed and coloured finishes for outdoor living areas, pool surrounds and entries.

  • 04

    Civil & Earthworks

    Excavation, site preparation, kerbing and drainage — the groundwork that decides how everything above it performs.
