What I Do

Web design and local SEO for Torquay, Geelong & the Surf Coast.

Most small business websites fail for the same two reasons. They load too slowly on a phone, and they don't make it obvious what the business does or how to get in touch. A visitor who has to work that out will just go back to Google and click the next result.

I build the opposite of that: hand-coded sites with no page-builder bloat, designed for a phone screen first, with the call-to-action where someone's thumb already is. Every build includes the technical groundwork search engines look for — clean heading structure, fast load times, structured data and a valid sitemap — so the site has a genuine chance of ranking rather than sitting on page four.

I'm based in Torquay and work with businesses across Geelong and the wider Surf Coast. That means I can meet you in person, and it means I understand how differently trade moves here between January and July.
