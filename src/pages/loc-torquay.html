What Torquay businesses usually need

The most common problem I see locally isn't an ugly website — it's an invisible one. A business has a perfectly decent site that nobody ever reaches, because the Google Business Profile is half-finished and the site itself has no local signals on it whatsoever.

The fix is usually unglamorous: get the profile properly claimed and categorised, make the website state plainly where you are and what you cover, keep the business details identical everywhere they appear, and start collecting reviews consistently. That combination moves the needle far more reliably than a redesign does.

Seasonal trade, and designing for it

If your revenue is concentrated in the warmer months, your website has two distinct jobs. In peak season it needs to convert holiday traffic fast — clear hours, obvious booking or phone contact, no friction for someone deciding on their phone at the beach. Out of season it needs to keep bringing in local trade, which is a different message to a different audience.

Most sites are only built for one of those. Building for both is straightforward once someone has actually thought about it.
