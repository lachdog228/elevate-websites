Before you email

What to include

None of this is compulsory — but the more you can tell us up front, the faster we can tell you something useful.

  • The brand and model. Graco or otherwise — whatever's on the badge.
  • What it's doing. Losing pressure, spitting, leaking, won't prime, running rough.
  • When it started. Sudden, gradual, or only under certain conditions.
  • How it's used. Daily production work or occasional jobs — it changes what to look for.
  • Your contact details. Name, business and a phone number if you'd rather talk it through.
