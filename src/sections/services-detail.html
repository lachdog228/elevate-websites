06 Services

Professional airless spray
equipment servicing

Whatever the gun is doing — losing pressure, spitting, leaking, refusing to prime — the job starts the same way: work out what's actually wrong before anything gets replaced.

  • 01

    Airless spray gun repairs

    Repair work to get airless spray guns operating correctly again — done carefully, with the aim of a gun that keeps working long after it leaves the bench.

  • 02

    Routine servicing

    Regular servicing for equipment in constant use. The cheapest repair is the one caught before it stops the job, so gear that gets worked hard is worth looking at between rather than during projects.

  • 03

    Warranty repairs

    Warranty repair work on eligible equipment. If your gear might still be covered, send through the brand, model and rough age and you'll be told where you stand before anything goes ahead.

  • 04

    Fault diagnosis

    Find the fault and work out what the equipment actually needs — including the intermittent problems that behave on the bench and play up on site. You get a clear account of it before work starts, so you're not paying for parts that were never the problem.

  • 05

    Genuine replacement parts

    Genuine replacement parts, fitted properly. Aftermarket seals and tips are cheaper on the invoice and more expensive by the end of the month — the parts that go in here are the ones the equipment was built for.

  • 06

    Performance testing

    Testing output and operation to confirm the equipment is performing as it should — before it goes back to you, and worth doing on its own ahead of a demanding job.
