## Notes for you, not for her

to your account — she'd hit a login wall. Deploy the folder to Netlify and send
that URL instead.

**Question 6 is the one that matters commercially.** If she wants the real four
soups on the site every day, someone has to update it every morning. That's
either a small monthly job for you, or a reason to put a simple editor on it so
she can do it from her phone. Worth settling before you quote the ongoing side.

consequences.
