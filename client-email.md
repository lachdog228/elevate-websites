## Notes for you, not for her

**Don't send the private preview link.** The Claude artifact preview is private
to your account, she'd hit a login wall. Deploy the folder to Netlify and send
that URL instead.

**Tell her to scroll slowly.** The opening sequence is the best thing on the
site and it's the one thing a client can miss entirely by flicking to the
bottom. Both versions of the email say it in the first line for that reason.

**Question 6 is the one that matters commercially.** If she wants the real four
soups on the site every day, someone has to update it every morning. That's
either a small monthly job for you, or a reason to put a simple editor on it so
she can do it from her phone. Worth settling before you quote the ongoing side.

**Lead with the dietary tags.** If she skims the whole email and only acts on
one thing, that's the one. Everything else is cosmetic, that one has actual
consequences.

**On the video.** If she does send real footage, it needs to be steady, shot
landscape, and framed with the cup toward the right of frame so the words still
have the left half of the screen. Ten seconds is plenty.
