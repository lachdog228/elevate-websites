Good to know

Trailer hire questions

Do I need to book a trailer in advance?

Booking ahead is the surest way to get the trailer you want on the day you want it. Send through your dates and we will tell you straight away what is free.

What do I need in order to tow a hire trailer?

A current licence, and a vehicle with a properly rated towbar plus the right coupling and electrics for the trailer. Tell us what you will be towing with when you enquire and we will confirm it suits before you book.

How long can I hire a trailer for?

Hire periods are confirmed when you enquire. Tell us how long you need it and we will let you know what works.

How much is trailer hire?

Pricing depends on the trailer and how long you need it. Call 0429 500 070 or send an enquiry and you will get a straight price before anything is booked.

Where do I pick the trailer up from?

621 Bellarine Hwy, Leopold VIC 3224 — on the highway between Geelong and the Bellarine. We will confirm a pickup time when we confirm the booking.
