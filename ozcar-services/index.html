Good to know

Common questions

Do I need to book a trailer in advance?

Booking ahead is the surest way to get the trailer you want on the day you want it. Send through your dates and we will tell you straight away what is free.

How much does trailer hire or towing cost?

It depends on what you are hiring or moving and how far it is going. Call 0429 500 070 or send an enquiry and you will get a straight price before anything is booked.

Do you tow after hours?

We are open 24 hours. If something has gone wrong late at night, call anyway — that is exactly what the number is there for.

What areas do you cover?

Leopold, Geelong and the Bellarine Peninsula as standard, plus the surrounding areas. Vehicle transport regularly goes further afield across Victoria — one of our customers had a car moved across the state.

What do I need in order to tow a hire trailer?

A current licence, and a vehicle with a properly rated towbar plus the right coupling and electrics for the trailer. Tell us what you will be towing with when you enquire and we will confirm it suits before you book.
